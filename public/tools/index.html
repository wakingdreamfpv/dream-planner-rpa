Set up logging on your radio

EdgeTX (RadioMaster, Jumper, TBS and others) with ExpressLRS. About five minutes.

  1. On the radio: Model › Telemetry › Discover new sensors with the quad powered, so GPS, battery, current and link sensors appear.
  2. Model › Special functions: add SF on your arm switch → SD Logs with an interval of 0.1–0.2 s.
  3. In the ExpressLRS Lua script set Telem ratio to 1:2 or 1:4 (faster GPS and height). Re-plug the quad after changing it.
  4. Fly. Each session lands on the SD card in /LOGS as a .csv named after the model.
  5. Copy the .csv to your computer and open it in HUD Studio or the Flight Log.
