Questions

Is the free planner really free?

Yes. There's no sign-up and no account. It keeps one job at a time on your device, and your exported PDF is your record. You can use it for paid work.

What do I give you for the beta?

Just your name, email and (if you like) your company name. No payment details. We'll email you about the beta and the paid launch, and we never share your details.

Where is my data kept?

In the free planner and the beta, on your own device only. Nothing is shared and nobody else can see it. Add the planner to your Home Screen and use Back up now and then, so your browser doesn't clear it.

What happens when the beta ends?

On 10 January 2027 you go back to the free planner unless you choose a paid plan. You can download everything you made in the beta, and bring it into a paid account.

Does it replace my CASA obligations?

No. The planner helps you prepare, approve and record your operations, but you and your Chief Remote Pilot remain responsible for meeting CASA's rules and your operations manual.
